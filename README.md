# NVIDIA (NVDA) Financial Analysis

An academic equity research project evaluating NVIDIA’s financial performance, stock trends, and long-term growth outlook as of November 2024.

**[View the financial analysis (PDF)](./Nvidia%20Presentation_Functions%20copy%20%283%29.pdf)**

## What I analyzed

| Area | Scope |
|---|---|
| Financial statements | Reviewed NVIDIA’s income statement and balance sheet, focusing on revenue growth, cost of revenue, liquidity, and leverage. |
| Stock performance | Compared historical stock performance over 3-, 5-, and 10-year periods using Fidelity charts dated November 24, 2024. |
| Valuation and risk | Examined the price-to-earnings ratio and beta to assess how much growth investors were pricing in and how sensitive the stock was to market moves. |
| Business drivers | Evaluated data center growth and competitive positioning alongside operating considerations such as inventory turnover and short-term debt. |

## Investment thesis at the time

The analysis identified data center expansion as a major driver of NVIDIA’s long-term growth. It also found that the stock’s premium valuation and elevated volatility made the case sensitive to whether NVIDIA could sustain that growth. The presentation brings these findings together into a historical investment thesis based on the information available in November 2024.
